CreatorGen AI

CreatorGen AI is a desktop application for YouTube creators. It runs entirely on your own computer and helps you research proven video topics, generate original scripts, produce videos with a built-in voice and renderer, design thumbnails, and publish to your own YouTube channel.

How it uses Google: when you connect your channel, CreatorGen AI uses Google sign-in (OAuth) only to identify your YouTube channel and to upload the videos you choose to publish. It uses the YouTube Data API with your own free API key for topic research. Your keys and sign-in tokens stay on your device.
