Privacy Policy — CreatorGen AI

CreatorGen AI is a desktop application that runs on your own computer.

Data stored on your device. Your projects, scripts, and settings are stored locally on your device. API keys you enter (YouTube, Groq, Pixabay) are stored on your device only, and Google sign-in tokens are stored in your operating system's secure keychain.

Google sign-in. When you connect your YouTube channel, the app uses Google sign-in only to identify your channel and to upload videos you choose to publish (scopes: youtube.upload and youtube.readonly).

Third-party services. When you use AI features, the text you generate is sent to Groq using your own API key. Stock photo searches are sent to Pixabay using your own API key. Topic research uses the YouTube Data API with your own API key.

No collection by the developer. The developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share your personal data, your content, or your keys. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

Your control. You can disconnect your channel and remove the app's stored data at any time from within the app, and revoke the app's Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: the support email listed on the app's Google Cloud consent screen.
